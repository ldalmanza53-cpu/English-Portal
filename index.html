Mission Stages

3:00 - 9:00

Phase 2: Negotiate the budget and integrate ideas.

Phase 3: Crisis! A major sponsor pulls out. Adapt.

13:00 - 15:00

Phase 4: Unanimous vote on the final festival plan.

Student A: The Music Director

Focus: Secure top-tier artists based on music success stories and general trends.

  • Discuss habits of successful bands.
  • Propose headline acts.

Student B: The Finance Lead

Focus: Bargain shopping, budget cuts, and managing financial logistics.

  • Advocate for budget-friendly vendor deals.
  • Use direct/indirect objects perfectly ("Send the sponsor an invoice").

Student C: Head of Conservation

Focus: Ensuring the festival protects local animal species and their habitats.

  • Classify local fauna at risk.
  • Establish strict environmental rules.

Student D: Venue Coordinator

Focus: Designing immersive nature experiences for festival attendees.

  • Propose outdoor activities (e.g., hiking, foraging).
  • Justify the psychological benefits of nature.
