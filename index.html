The Anniversary Party

15:00

Main Task & Scenario Overview

You are three siblings planning your parents' anniversary party. You need to buy a gift and plan an activity. However, you all have different ideas and information about the rest of the family.

Final Deliverable

You must reach a 100% consensus on a 3-point action plan recorded in the Group Scratchpad before 0:00. The plan must include:

  • The selected gift and how much it costs.
  • The main family activity and how many people are participating.
  • What other family members are doing right now to prepare.
0-3m: Read Roles 3-8m: Discuss Activities 8-13m: Negotiate Gifts 13-15m: Write Consensus

Sibling 1: The Organizer

You want everything to be perfect and have a specific gift in mind.

Secret Motives

  • You want to buy this watch. It costs $50.
  • You think $50 is a good price.
  • You know that a lot of cousins love dancing.
  • Right now: "My aunt is cooking dinner."

Sibling 2: The Budgeter

You want to save money and prefer a quiet family activity.

Secret Motives

  • You want to buy that photo album. It costs $20.
  • You think $50 is too expensive.
  • You know that nearly all relatives want to play board games.
  • Right now: "My grandparents are sleeping."

Sibling 3: The Entertainer

You want a big celebration and don't care about the price.

Secret Motives

  • You want to buy those concert tickets. They cost $100.
  • You think the album is boring.
  • You know that few people like board games.
  • Right now: "My uncles are decorating the house."
