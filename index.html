Eco-Harmony Festival Committee

15:00

Main Task & Scenario Overview

The Scenario

Your team is finalizing the plans for the largest outdoor environmental event of the year: The Eco-Harmony Festival. You must successfully combine a headline musical performance, a bustling bargain-shopping eco-market, and an interactive wildlife sanctuary—all while ensuring attendees feel a deep, genuine connection to nature. Your conflicting departmental interests threaten to derail the event.

Concrete Deliverable

You must reach a 100% consensus on a 3-point action plan recorded in the Group Scratchpad before 0:00:

  • The exact musical act to book and how to handle their habits.
  • The organizational strategy for the bargain market to ensure vendor profits.
  • The specific animal categories to feature in the wildlife sanctuary.

15-Minute Timeline

  • 15:00

    Phase 1: Initial Pitches. Each role presents their non-negotiable demands.

  • 10:00

    Phase 2: Deep Negotiation. Argue and compromise over space, budget, and festival vibes.

  • 05:00

    Phase 3: Final Drafting. Type your agreed 3-point plan into the scratchpad below.

Committee Roles (Tap/Click to reveal secrets)

The Booking Agent

In charge of securing a music success story to headline the festival.

Tap to Flip

Secret Backstory

You owe a massive favor to an eccentric rock band. You must convince the committee to book them over a peaceful acoustic artist.

Conflicting Motive

The rock band is completely anti-nature. They demand high energy and loud volume.

Target Language Checklist

  • Use will for habits: "The lead singer will always demand plastic water bottles..."
  • Use would for past truths: "In the past, they would refuse to play outdoors."

Market Director

Manages the vendors, stalls, and overall shopping experience.

Tap to Flip

Secret Backstory

You promised major corporate sponsors the best spots for aggressive bargain shopping. You care about profit, not trees.

Conflicting Motive

You need the animals and the music moved to the edges so the shopping stalls are center-stage.

Target Language Checklist

  • Use Direct/Indirect Objects (Pattern 1): "We must give the sponsors a discount."
  • Use Direct/Indirect Objects (Pattern 2): "I sent the contract to the vendors."

Wildlife Coordinator

Designs the animal sanctuary exhibit and educational displays.

Tap to Flip

Secret Backstory

You are obsessed with endangered reptiles and amphibians. You refuse to feature popular mammals (like monkeys or tigers).

Conflicting Motive

The market noise will stress the amphibians. You want the market downsized to protect their habitat.

Target Language Checklist

  • Use classification language: "This species falls under the category of..."
  • Describe features: "Amphibians are characterized by their porous skin, whereas mammals..."

Environmental Liaison

Guarantees the entire festival remains deeply connected to nature.

Tap to Flip

Secret Backstory

You despise commercialism, loud rock music, and captive animals. You want a silent, trade-only rustic retreat.

Conflicting Motive

You have the legal power to shut down any vendor or band that isn't 100% "off-the-grid".

Target Language Checklist

  • Use connection expressions: "We need to be at one with nature..."
  • "This market severs our ties to the natural world." / "We must immerse ourselves in the wilderness."
