The 10-Year High School Reunion

You are 5 old friends. Today, you meet again after 10 years!

Concrete Final Deliverable

Work together to write The Reunion Report in the Group Scratchpad below. You must agree on:

  • The biggest change in someone's life (What did they use to do?).
  • One good piece of advice for your past selves.
  • One interesting thing another classmate said today.

Mission Stages Breakdown

Stage 1

Remember the Past

Talk about your early life. Discuss what you used to do when you were young.

Stage 2

Imagine & Advise

Talk about imaginary situations, and give your friends advice about their old mistakes.

Stage 3

Report & Agree

Tell the group what other people said (rumors), and write your final report together.

Choose Your Role (Tap to view secret)

The Nostalgic

You remember everything about your early life.

Secret Backstory

You miss the past. You want to talk about the simple life you had when you were young.

Target Language:

  • Use the simple past.
  • Talk about a childhood memory.
  • Use "was" and "were".

The Changed One

Your habits are completely different now.

Secret Backstory

You used to be very quiet. Now you are very loud and famous. Compare your old life!

Target Language:

  • Compare past and current habits.
  • Use "used to" (e.g., I used to play...).
  • Use "didn't use to".

The Dreamer

You always think about imaginary events.

Secret Backstory

You wonder how life would be if things were different. You like to dream about the impossible.

Target Language:

  • Discuss imaginary events.
  • Use "If I were..." or "If I had...".
  • Ask others about their dreams.

The Advisor

You like giving advice on past mistakes.

Secret Backstory

You think you know best. You always tell people what they did wrong in the past and suggest better ideas.

Target Language:

  • Give advice about past events.
  • Use "You should have..." or "It was a bad idea to...".
  • Ask for advice too.

The Reporter

You love sharing what others said.

Secret Backstory

You spoke to other old classmates before the reunion. You want to repeat their statements to the group.

Target Language:

  • Discuss statements other people made.
  • Use reported speech ("He said that...").
  • Mention opinions from old teachers.
