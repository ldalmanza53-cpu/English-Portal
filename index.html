The PR Crisis: Operation Wild Recovery

Level: C1 | Students: 4 | Time: 15 Minutes

15:00

🔥 Group Mission & Instructions

Context: A massive scandal has destroyed the reputation of "The Sonic Waves", a globally successful band. You are their PR Management Team. To fix their image, you must launch an "Eco-Recovery Campaign" before the press conference starts in 15 minutes.

The Final Outcome: You must reach a unanimous decision on ONE complete campaign package. If you fail to agree, the band will be dropped by their label.


Phase Breakdown:

  • Phase 1: Pitching (4 min) - Each manager proposes their strategy using their role's specific angles.
  • Phase 2: The Negotiation (7 min) - Debate, dissect, and compromise. Critique other managers' ideas constructively.
  • Phase 3: The Verdict (4 min) - Vote and finalize the exact Animal Category, Nature Activity, and Charity Auction strategy.
