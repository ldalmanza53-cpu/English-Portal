The Airport Security Check

You are at the airport. You need to check the IDs of 3 people.

Your Group Mission

Work together. Write the ID Check Form in the Group Scratchpad below. You need:

  • Person 1: Name and Country
  • Person 2: Name and Job
  • Person 3: Name and Age

Mission Stages

Stage 1

Introduce

Say hello. Introduce yourself and others using "I am" and "He/She is".

Stage 2

Ask & Confirm

Ask yes/no questions to confirm information. Use "Are you...?" or "Is he...?"

Stage 3

Write IDs

Write the correct information in the Group Scratchpad.

Choose Your Role

The Friend

You know everyone. You like to introduce people.

Your Information

You are Tom. You are from Canada. Introduce yourself and the people next to you.

Target Language:

  • 👉 "Hello, I am Tom."
  • 👉 "I am from Canada."
  • 👉 "This is my friend."

The Officer

You work at the airport. You ask many questions.

Your Information

You have a paper with mistakes. You think Tom is from the UK. You think the doctor is a teacher.

Target Language:

  • 👉 "Are you from the UK?"
  • 👉 "Is he a teacher?"
  • 👉 "Are you 25 years old?"

The Doctor

You are tired. You only answer yes or no.

Your Information

You are Anna. You are 30 years old. You are a doctor. Correct the Officer's mistakes.

Target Language:

  • 👉 "No, I am not."
  • 👉 "Yes, I am."
  • 👉 "I am a doctor."
