Family Weekend

Main Task & Scenario

You are a family. Today is Friday. You want to do a family activity this weekend. But activities cost money! Talk about your jobs, look at the prices, and choose one activity together.

Activities List:

  • Cinema: $15 per person
  • Park & Picnic: $5 per person
  • Museum: $10 per person

Mission Stages:

  1. Say your job and how much money you have.
  2. Ask your family: "What activity do you want?"
  3. Compare prices (e.g., "The park is cheap").
  4. Decide together and write it in the Group Scratchpad.
Final Deliverable: Reach a consensus on 1 simple family activity and write it down below.

Player 1: The Doctor

You work in a hospital.

Secret Motive

You are a doctor. You have $40. You are tired. You want a quiet and relaxing activity (Museum).

Checklist:

  • Say: "I am a doctor."
  • Ask: "What is your job?"
  • Compare: "The museum is cheaper than the cinema."

Player 2: The Chef

You work in a restaurant.

Secret Motive

You are a chef. You have $10. You don't have much money. You want a cheap activity (Park).

Checklist:

  • Say: "I am a chef."
  • Ask: "How much is the park?"
  • Compare: "The cinema is very expensive."

Player 3: The Student

You study at the university.

Secret Motive

You don't have a job, you are a student. You have $20. You love movies. You want to go to the cinema.

Checklist:

  • Say: "I am a student."
  • Ask: "Do you like the cinema?"
  • Compare: "The cinema is better than the park."

Player 4: The Artist

You work with photos and paint.

Secret Motive

You are an artist. You have $30. You like art, but you also like food. You are happy with Museum or Picnic.

Checklist:

  • Say: "I am an artist."
  • Ask: "What do you want to do?"
  • Compare: "The park is good, but the museum is nice too."
